This profile is made up, and so is Mark Dalton — it is the same composed scenario the course What You Post About Work, and Who Is Reading It uses, not a real person’s account. Nothing here is drawn from anyone’s profile, and the byte stores nothing: refresh the page and it starts over.

Deliberately not included: the settings paths for LinkedIn, Facebook or anywhere else. Those move every few months, and the course already gives the three that matter — your connection list, your post rule, and the apps you connected years ago. What this byte adds is the judgement that has to come first, because “make everything private” is not a decision anyone with a career can actually follow.
