Defence Lab

Practical Cybersecurity for the Modern Workplace

Security awareness training for people who don't work in security. Two formats: Courses build the why — a real incident, taken apart step by step, 4–7 minutes. Security Bytes give the how — a tool or a card you keep, 2–4 minutes. Both stand alone, and both stack into a path.

This is a live preview. Bytes are numbered after the course they pair with — Byte 6b belongs with Course 6 — so each one sits next to the material it extends. Items marked planned are written into the catalogue but not built yet. Content and design are in active development.
